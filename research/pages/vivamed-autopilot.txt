 VivaMed Autopilot
 A fact-checked health Shorts channel that runs itself: PubMed-only scripts, two-tier review, daily posting, and a nightly loop that learns which hooks work.
 Like
 Comment
 Story 
 Updates 
 Submission history 
 Inspiration
 Health myths spread fastest in short video, and the accounts that correct them burn out: researching, scripting, recording and posting every day is a full-time job. I am a medical student. I wanted a channel that corrects myths with real studies every day without needing me every day.
 What it does
 VivaMed Autopilot runs a fact-checked health Shorts channel end to end:
 Drafts a "myth or fact" script only from real PubMed abstracts, after a relevance gate checks that each abstract actually studies the question. Fewer than two relevant papers means no script.
 A reviewer model checks every claim against its cited paper; a stronger model makes the final call to approve or reject it.
 Renders narration, captions and visuals locally, then posts two Shorts a day to YouTube, Instagram and TikTok at a human pace.
 Every night it reads the views of every Short and feeds the best-performing hooks back into the next scripts. Evidence rules never loosen: only the hook style adapts.
 A watchdog checks every job hourly, re-runs anything that failed, and writes one status page.
 What existed before, and what is new in this event
 Before: the PubMed-grounded script writer and the video renderer.
 New during LaunchPad Hacks: the analytics feedback loop, the dynamic posting queue (two a day per platform), the two-tier review gate (cheap models check, a stronger model decides), and the self-healing watchdog.
 How we built it
 Python, free cloud models through a local router, local text-to-speech and Whisper timing, HTML-to-video rendering, Windows Task Scheduler for jobs, and a logged-in browser for posting at a human pace. It runs on one mini PC at almost no cost per video.
 Challenges
 Making it survive reboots and silent failures: jobs started by hand died with the session, so every job became an idempotent scheduled task with a watchdog.
 AI disclosure
 Scripts are drafted by AI models from real PubMed abstracts and checked before publishing. The code was written with an AI coding assistant.
 Limitations
 Translations and scripts are not reviewed by a practising clinician yet. This is health education, not medical advice.
