 Hi 
 Inspiration
 Our hackathon was long. Our original idea was a interior design app built on Project Tango for homeowners to send depth perception models of their rooms in 3D, and then have home designers drag and drop 3D objects into these homes with buy buttons sourced from APIs. 
 This proved to be much too hard for a 24 hour hack. So we decided to pivot in a way that would be enjoyable for all and allow us to learn all those funky JS frameworks we 'lurve.
 Other side hacks we built was a python based HPE Enterprise Maven API hack to analyse Hillary Clinton's emails via NLP/sentiment analysis on D3, and a Phaser.JS based game to land an Elon Musk rocket onto a Cape Carnaveral launchpad all within the confines of your friendly monitor.
 We decided to build something fun for Hack Cambridge, given that the World Economic Forum representatives and delegates would be in session. We also wanted to draw attention to the refugee crisis we face globally in a fun and witty way. So we decided to hack around with Trumpscript. 
 What it does
 Did Trump Offend Me is a parody of Donald Trump's campaign. Donald Trump has offended many people this year , across all groups and factions. 
 Using our app, you can answer a simple quiz about yourself, and we can let you know whether you should be offended by Donald's actions, or whether he is your friend or not. If you have been offended, fear not, you can donate to the Syrian refugee crisis , as we all know how important that is for !President Trump to get more foreigners into the US. In addition, we will ping you via the Mailjet API to let you know how much Donald Trump doesn't like your face.
 How we built it
 Angular 1, Express, Mongoose, Node (MEAN). Copying styles from Trump's very own.
 Challenges we ran into
 Learning React in a hackathon is hard. Its too tiring to think.
 Hacking for fun makes you learn more than a business idea
 Project Tango isn't ready yet, and isn't much better than Oculus or a VR headset
 Accomplishments that we're proud of
 Giving back to the world, and giving Donald Trump 400 more supporters, and raising awareness about Donald Trump => more donations to Donald Trump's campaign (which is actually the Syrian Refugee crisis).
 What we learned
 How to hack in a team in a banter-friendly way.
 What's next for trump-offender-quiz
 Analyse Hillary's emails and spit out random phrases to shout at Donald Trump
 Be able to land a rocket using Project Tango and a floating dinosaur
 Dodge Donald Trump asteroids using a SpaceX rocket, batting away the enemy.
