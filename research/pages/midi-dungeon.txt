 MIDI Dungeon
 A top-down dungeon crawler that utilizes a Launchpad MIDI controller for user control and display functionality.
 Like
 Comment
 Story 
 Updates 
 Submission history 
 Inspiration
 Undertale and EDM production.
 What it does
 It dungeon-crawls and makes use of a MIDI controller.
 How we built it
 From the ground up.
 Challenges we ran into
 Compiler errors, non-compiler errors, ineptitude, not being full stack developers, x/y confusion, lack of GitHub literacy, lack of literacy.
 Accomplishments that we're proud of
 Teamwork.
 What we learned
 Stuff about mythical critters, T-Pain, and more than anything, ourselves.
