 BlinkTalk AI
 An AI-powered assistive communication tool that lets users select words with eye blinks and turns them into smart sentence suggestions and speech.
 Like
 1 
 Comment
 Story 
 Updates 
 Submission history 
 Inspiration
 People living with ALS, stroke, or severe paralysis often keep full mental clarity but lose the ability to speak or use their hands. Dedicated eye-tracking communication devices exist, but they are often very expensive and out of reach for many families. We asked: what if any laptop with a webcam could become a voice?
 What it does
 BlinkTalk AI lets a person communicate using only eye blinks and a regular webcam — no special hardware, no installation.
 The webcam detects intentional blinks in real time.
 A scanning keyboard highlights letters and quick phrases; a blink selects one.
 An AI layer predicts what the user wants to say. Typing just "wa" can suggest "I'm thirsty, can I have some water?" — turning dozens of blinks into a few.
 The selected sentence is spoken aloud with text-to-speech.
 An emergency phrase ("I need help") is always one blink away.
 How we built it
 MediaPipe Face Landmarker runs in the browser to track facial landmarks and blink scores.
 Blink detection uses a calibrated threshold plus a minimum duration, so natural blinks are ignored and only deliberate long blinks count.
 [LLM API name] generates context-aware sentence suggestions from partial input.
 Web Speech API reads sentences aloud.
 Everything runs as a static web app in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript ; video never leaves the user's device.
