 Pomsafe
 Open Source Home Automation and Security
 Like
 Comment
 Story 
 Updates 
 Submission history 
 Pomsafe is an open source home security and automation platform running off of a TI Launchpad, powered by Firebase. We offer a cheap, lower power IOT solution to home security that can be installed on a room by room basis. When our module is attached to a door, we allow real time monitoring of room tempurature, open doors, and possible breakins.
